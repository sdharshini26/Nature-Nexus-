Nature Nexus

Natural Alternatives for Sustainable Packaging

1. Project Overview

Nature Nexus is a sustainable packaging concept that explores the use of natural and biodegradable materials as alternatives to conventional packaging.

2. Problem Statement

Conventional packaging can contribute to plastic pollution and long-lasting waste. Nature Nexus explores plant-based materials as potential alternatives to reduce packaging waste.

3. Proposed Solution

The concept investigates a biodegradable package made using banana stem fibre, coconut husk pulp and starch-based materials. It also explores embedding viable seeds into selected parts of the package to encourage planting after use.

4. Proposed Materials

- Kraft paper: Base material for the compostable snack packaging pouch.
- Starch-based material: Used as part of the proposed barrier layer.
- Chitosan: Proposed as a component of the protective barrier coating.
- Sorbitol: Used as a plasticiser to help reduce brittleness and improve flexibility.
- Natural wax: Proposed as an inner layer to improve the package’s moisture resistance.
- Guar gum: Plant-derived adhesive for attaching seeds to the seed strips.
- Cellulose paper or seed paper: Carrier material for the seed strips.
- Viable seeds: Seeds such as tulsi, marigold, fenugreek, or coriander, selected for the seed-embedded packaging concept.
- Water: Used as the hydration medium for preparing the guar-gum adhesive.

The final material composition, food-contact suitability, moisture resistance, compostability, and seed viability must be evaluated through appropriate testing before making performance claims.

5. Key Features

- Nature-inspired packaging design
- Exploration of plant-based raw materials
- Potential reduction in conventional packaging waste
- Seed-embedded design concept
- Focus on sustainability and responsible material use

6. Project Status

Current stage: Product design and concept development.

A functional prototype, biodegradability, seed viability, material strength and safety have not yet been confirmed.

7. Technical Documentation

Technical design, proposed material specifications, sample data and test plans will be documented in the "docs", "sample-data" and "testing" folders.

8. Future Work

- Develop a physical prototype
- Evaluate material strength and moisture resistance
- Test biodegradation under defined conditions
- Assess seed germination and viability
- Refine the packaging design based on results
